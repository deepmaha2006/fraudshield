2. Data we collect

  • Account data: full name, e-mail address, phone number (optional).
  • Authentication data: bcrypt-hashed password, session tokens (opaque, stored server-side).
  • Identity verification data: document type, document number (stored as a one-way hash), name and date of birth as they appear on the document. [CONFIRM: is manual ID verification in use?]
  • Detection data: URLs, message text, and screenshots you submit for analysis; analysis verdicts and reports.
  • Gmail integration: Gmail App Password (stored AES-256-GCM encrypted), e-mail metadata (sender, subject, links) of messages your Gmail account receives. We do not store e-mail body content beyond what is needed to produce a detection report.
  • SMS integration: SMS sender and message body forwarded from the FraudShield Android app.
  • Technical data: IP address and user-agent recorded with each session; server logs.

3. How we use your data

  • To authenticate you and maintain your session.
  • To analyse URLs, messages, and e-mails for fraud and phishing on your behalf.
  • To send you high-risk alert e-mails (if enabled in your settings).
  • To send transactional e-mails (password reset, e-mail verification).
  • To provide usage statistics visible in your dashboard.
